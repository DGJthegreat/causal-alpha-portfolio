Corrections, Oct 2026

Earlier versions of this site reported a Sharpe ratio of 1.26 for this strategy. That figure used 2x leverage while charging trading costs as if the position were 1x; the bug is fixed and covered by a test. The corrected figure that replaced it was measured on data that had helped choose the rule, so it is withdrawn too. The figures on this page come from a pre-registered test: the rules were frozen and committed before the test sectors were downloaded.

What this is

CausalAlpha was built on five NSE sector indices: Bank Nifty, Nifty IT, Nifty Energy, Nifty FMCG and Nifty Pharma. Under its headline rule, VIX_GATE, each sector is held only while its own 20-day momentum is positive and India VIX is at or below its 20-day median; otherwise that sector's slot sits in cash.

The fair test was run on three sectors it never saw while it was being built (Nifty Auto, Metal and Realty), from 2021 onward, after trading costs. Every approach was judged on the same data, timing and costs, and none beat simply holding those sectors. Its shallower drawdowns came from holding less of the time, not from timing.

The other ideas fared no better. Reinforcement-learning agents (a GTrXL transformer and an MLP, five seeds each) did no better than plain momentum. The causal links found by PCMCI did not survive a rank-based test. A monthly Nifty straddle failed out-of-sample and is frozen.

Methodology

01

One information set, one execution rule

Every input is used only once it was public at that day's close (US closes and evening flow data count from the next day). A decision taken at one close is traded at the next close, idle cash earns the RBI repo rate, and NSE charges plus half the bid-ask spread are paid on every trade.

02

Many strategies tested, counted

Every variant tried during design was counted. The deflated Sharpe ratio, Hansen's SPA test and the probability of backtest overfitting all use that count, so luck across many attempts cannot pass as skill.

03

Pre-registered, on untouched sectors

The rules and the test were frozen and committed in git before the three test sectors were downloaded, and a script checks that order. The design sectors' 2023 onward results were used during design, so they are reported as reused, never as out-of-sample.

Strategy status

Paper trading

VIX_GATE, unlevered

A forward log records its frozen decisions every trading day. The pre-registered test found no edge over buy-and-hold, so no real capital is allocated.

Failed, frozen

Nifty monthly straddle

Failed out-of-sample and its parameter grid showed the edge is not robust (research log section 16.7). Frozen. Any straddle shown on the Live page is a paper record only.

Experimental

ADX+MOM20 blended portfolio

Experimental, in-sample, not validated. Its ADX threshold was grid-searched on the same data used to score it, and the blend included the failed straddle. Earlier headline figures based on it are withdrawn.
