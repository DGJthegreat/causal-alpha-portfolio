Every approach on the untouched sectors, after costs
Universe

NSE sector indices

Built on five Nifty sector indices (Bank, IT, Energy, FMCG, Pharma); judged on three it never saw (Auto, Metal, Realty). Benchmark: buy and hold of the same sectors, on the same trading days.

Rule

Per-sector momentum, volatility gate

Five equal slots, one per sector. A slot holds its sector only while that sector's 20-day momentum is positive and India VIX is at or below its 20-day median; otherwise the slot is in cash. Nothing is ranked. Decisions use data up to the previous close only.

Costs and leverage

Unlevered, 0.2% each way

Headline figures are 1x. Reinforcement-learning agents trained for the same task found no edge and are not part of this rule.
